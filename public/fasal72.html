About this demo

Fasal72 is a hackathon prototype that demonstrates a claims agent that plans its own next step, uses tools, remembers demo state, and stops for a human before filing.

What is real

  • Open-Meteo weather data when the live-weather toggle is enabled; results are a coarse reanalysis/forecast grid.
  • Browser speech recognition when supported by your browser and permissions.
  • In-app reminder timers, calendar (.ics) downloads, and demo state saved in this browser.

What is mocked

  • PMFBY policy records, farmer and plot details.
  • Photo analysis, portal submission and acknowledgement.
  • Claim tracking, surveyor visit and settlement stages.

Honest limit

Weather-grid data cannot prove hail on a single field. A storm signal is not proof of damage. The packet is limited to its displayed evidence grade. No real government portal is contacted and no real personal data is requested.

Language and control

Choose Marathi, Hindi or English for farmer-facing messages. The RUKO / STOP control halts the planner immediately; a claim is only sent to the mock portal after the farmer presses “Yes, submit”.
